Four editorial guides, ten courses each. Operator and public-agency sources are linked in each entry. Prices are published references; dynamic and resort-package rates are not guessed. Value means a mix of low-cost rounds and design-focused upgrades, not a statewide cheapest-price ranking.

- The Bog: operator seasonal-rate page retains a 2023 URL; describe the current displayed schedule as posted rates, not a newly issued 2026 rate card. Late-season cart inclusion is not explicit, so readers must confirm it. Twilight prime-season prices are walking and cart is optional. 5.5% sales tax extra.

- Milwaukee County: standard prices separated from discount-card rates; carts extra. Day-specific weather notices are not promises of future availability.
- Sand Valley: walking-only and tax-exclusive terms verified; quote required rather than deriving a price from inaccessible rate artwork.
