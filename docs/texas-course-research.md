# Texas course guides — October 15, 2026

Two research-based editorial shortlists with ten mapped entries each. Operator and city rate sources are linked in every article section. Prices are references rather than guaranteed quotes.

- Public versus resort access: Fields Ranch explicitly permits public tee times on East and West, with a seven-day public window. East currently lists required caddies and no carts September 18–November 29. Barton Creek is registered resort guests/members only. TPC San Antonio requires eligible JW Marriott guests or members. Houston Tournament Course explicitly welcomes public bookings; Member Course does not.
- Lost Pines Golf Club is the current name used by the resort and Bastrop County tourism listing; formerly Wolfdancer. Do not publish an old Wolfdancer rate as current.
- Ordinary daily-fee sources: Pine Dunes prices include cart and range balls; Rawls uses dynamic pricing. Lajitas directs callers to seasonal quotes.
- Value pricing uses ordinary adult public fees, not resident, annual-member or senior/junior discounts. GolfATX fees are green fees plus a separate cart charge. Gus Wortham taxes and optional priority-booking fees are extra. Alamo City rates distinguish public/passholder and walking/riding columns. Dallas course operators show newer rate tables than the generic parks fee page; use the course operator and confirm checkout totals. Meadowbrook 2026 Canyon rates include tax and fees; weekend prepayment and walking telephone-booking rules apply. Butterfield’s separate course-information page explicitly includes carts in the green fee; its posted rate schedule adds tax.
