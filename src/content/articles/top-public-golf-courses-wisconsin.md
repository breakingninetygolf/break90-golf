Wisconsin can turn a golf trip into a tour of very different landscapes: Lake Michigan shoreline, inland dunes, rolling championship ground and classic parkland golf. These are our ten public-access picks when the experience matters more than finding the lowest green fee.

These are Break 90 Golf’s research-based editorial selections, not an official ranking or firsthand condition review. We considered course character, booking access and the variety of the golf experience. Separate layouts at one property appear individually.

**Rates and access checked October 15, 2026.** Prices are USD per player unless stated otherwise. Posted rates are references, not guaranteed quotes. Taxes, carts, caddies, gratuities, park entry and lodging are extra unless explicitly included. Where the operator does not publish a dependable current flat rate, we say **quote required** rather than invent a price. Confirm maintenance, seasonal closure and cancellation terms before traveling.

Compare our [Wisconsin best-value guide](/articles/best-value-public-golf-courses-wisconsin/), or browse all [state course guides](/courses/).

**Season planning:** Wisconsin’s season is weather-dependent. Late tee times may not leave enough daylight for 18 holes, and a published rate does not mean the course is open today. Check frost delays, aeration and closing dates directly.

## 1. Whistling Straits — Straits Course

**Sheboygan / Haven**

Pete Dye’s Lake Michigan layout is the headline choice for a special-occasion Wisconsin round. The exposed shoreline setting and major-championship history make the experience different from an ordinary parkland outing. Choose it for the setting and challenge, with enough time and energy for a walking round.

**Price:** **Live green-fee quote required.** Walking only; an optional caddie is $90 per player, with a $70-per-bag suggested gratuity, plus applicable tax.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Choose comfortable tees and aim away from the lakeside trouble. A photo-worthy flag does not have to be your target.

[Official course information](https://www.kohlerwisconsin.com/golf/whistling-straits) · [Official rates / booking](https://www.kohlerwisconsin.com/golf/golf-rates--safety-guidelines-and-policies)

## 2. Erin Hills

**Erin**

Rolling ground gives Erin Hills a very different personality from the lakeshore. Its broad landscape can disguise demanding approach distances, making this a strong choice for golfers who enjoy choosing a route rather than simply firing at a visible target.

**Price:** **2026 green fee: $495 May 26–September 27; $395 May 4–25 and September 28–October 17.** Caddie service is $65 per player with a suggested $75+ gratuity. The course is generally walking only.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Account for uneven lies and the effort of walking. Pick tees for your reliable carry distance, not the championship reputation.

[Official course information](https://erinhills.com/golf/) · [Official rates / booking](https://erinhills.com/golf/rates-fees/)

## 3. Sand Valley

**Nekoosa**

The original Coore and Crenshaw course uses central Wisconsin’s sand landscape for multiple ways into a hole. Wide views do not remove the need to choose angles. It belongs on a trip built around firm-ground strategy and an immersive walking experience.

**Price:** **Date-specific quote required** from the operator’s 2026 rates/booking page. Rates exclude tax; the course is walking only. Confirm caddie costs and golf-only availability.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Consider where the ball will finish after landing. A run-up option can be more sensible than forcing a high carry.

[Official course information](https://www.sandvalley.com/golf-courses/sand-valley) · [Official rates / booking](https://www.sandvalley.com/golf-courses/rates)

## 4. Mammoth Dunes

**Nekoosa**

David McLay Kidd’s design takes scale seriously, with expansive fairways and greens. That makes it a compelling counterpoint to tighter courses on this list. Finding the putting surface is only the start when your first putt may be exceptionally long.

**Price:** **Date-specific quote required** through Sand Valley’s official rates and booking page. Walking only; tax and caddie costs require separate confirmation.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Warm up with longer putts. Aim for the correct section of the green rather than treating every green hit as the same outcome.

[Official course information](https://www.sandvalley.com/golf-courses/mammoth-dunes) · [Official rates / booking](https://www.sandvalley.com/golf-courses/rates)

## 6. Blackwolf Run — River Course

**Kohler**

The River Course is the inland counterpart to Whistling Straits, with Pete Dye’s routing using the Sheboygan River environment. It is a worthwhile choice for a second major round near Kohler when you want a contrasting landscape rather than another shoreline experience.

**Price:** **Live green-fee quote required.** The 2026 operator schedule lists a $50-per-person cart fee; confirm inclusions and any forecaddie charges before paying.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Take the river out of play when you can. A conservative recovery can be worth more than a speculative approach.

## 10. Lawsonia — Links Course

**Green Lake**

Lawsonia’s Links gives this shortlist a classic-design counterweight to large resort complexes. It is a destination in its own right and a useful stop for golfers interested in the shape and placement of greens, not just a dramatic backdrop.

## Plan a round that fits your trip

Keep the travel sensible: pair Kohler/Sheboygan rounds together, use Green Lake for Lawsonia, and leave a separate block for Nekoosa or Stevens Point. The value list is deliberately strongest around Milwaukee and southeastern Wisconsin; it is not an evenly distributed statewide budget directory. Compare walking totals with riding totals, and do not use a resident-card price as your visitor budget.

Before booking, ask about green aeration, the tees and holes currently available, cart restrictions, the full checkout total and cancellation terms. Confirm that the booking is for the course named here when a property has multiple layouts.

For your score, use the [math of breaking 90](/articles/break-90-golf-scoring-math-find-lost-strokes/) and choose tees that fit your normal carry distance. A memorable round does not require playing the back tees.
