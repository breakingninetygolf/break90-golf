## Start with your grip

For a right-handed golfer, rotating the lead (left) hand slightly clockwise on the grip can strengthen it and may help the face close sooner. Reverse directions if you play left-handed.

## Make small adjustments

A stronger grip is not a universal cure. Clubface, path and contact all affect a slice.

## Watch the flight

Try one small change at a time at the range. If the ball hooks sharply, you may have overdone it.

## Keep it playable

Use a conservative target on the course and save major changes for practice.
