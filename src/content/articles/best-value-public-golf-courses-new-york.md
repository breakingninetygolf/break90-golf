New York has an unusually useful public-golf menu: state parks, county courses and resort-area options that do not all carry a resort-size fee. The strongest value often comes from choosing the right season and walking when practical. This guide includes nine full-round destinations and one clearly marked nine-hole option.

This is Break 90 Golf’s research-based editorial shortlist, using course-operator and public-agency information. It is not an official statewide ranking or a firsthand review. We select for course character, accessible public play and a useful price proposition. Separate layouts at the same property may appear individually.

**Rates and booking information checked October 15, 2026.** All prices are USD per player unless identified as per cart. They are published references, not guaranteed quotes. Taxes, booking fees, travel, lodging and gratuities are extra unless explicitly included. Seasonal rates apply only to the dates shown; availability is weather-dependent. Where a dependable adult price could not be confirmed, we link to a live quote instead of estimating.

Looking for a special-occasion round? See our [New York no-budget-limit shortlist](/articles/top-public-golf-courses-new-york/).

## 1. Green Lakes State Park Golf Course

**Fayetteville · Best for scenery and affordable public golf**

This early Robert Trent Jones design uses rolling terrain, uneven lies and deep bunkers. It offers more to consider than a cheap tee time alone, with the surrounding state park making a longer visit possible.

**Price:** Published regular green fees **$22 weekday / $32 weekend and holiday**, plus **$18 per player for a cart**. The official **September 21-to-close fall special is $20 walking / $38 riding daily**.

**Break 90 approach:** Check the slope under your feet before choosing a club. Aim for the center when an uneven lie makes a precise approach unreliable.

[Official course, rates and booking](https://parks.ny.gov/visit/golf-courses/green-lakes-state-park-golf-course)

## 2. Bethpage — Blue Course

**Farmingdale · Best for an affordable route into Bethpage**

Blue is one of the complex’s Tillinghast-designed layouts. It offers a full round at Bethpage without the higher nonresident pricing attached to Black and Red.

**Price:** **$38 weekday / $43 weekend and holiday green fee** on the April 2026 sheet, plus reservation and optional cart charges. See the shared Bethpage booking notes below.

**Break 90 approach:** Choose your own targets and tees rather than letting the complex’s reputation dictate how you play.

[Official complex overview](https://parks.ny.gov/visit/golf-courses/bethpage-state-park-golf-courses) · [2026 fees](https://parks.ny.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/Bethpage%20State%20Park%20Golf%20Price%20List.pdf)

## 3. Bethpage — Green Course

**Farmingdale · Best for a different Bethpage layout at the same fee**

Green developed from the original Lenox Hills course, later modified by Tillinghast. It makes a useful second choice when comparing the three lower-priced 18-hole layouts at the complex.

**Price:** The same **$38/$43 standard green fee** as Blue and Yellow, before reservation and optional cart charges.

**Break 90 approach:** Keep the plan simple on unfamiliar holes: a comfortable tee club, a clear landing area and a safe approach.

[Official history and course information](https://parks.ny.gov/visit/golf-courses/bethpage-state-park-golf-courses) · [2026 fees](https://parks.ny.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/Bethpage%20State%20Park%20Golf%20Price%20List.pdf)

## 4. Bethpage — Yellow Course

**Farmingdale · Best for another full-round option at a public fee**

Alfred Tull’s Yellow completes our Bethpage value trio. These are separate courses, not three entries for the same round; choose by availability and the course experience you want.

**Price:** **$38 weekday / $43 weekend and holiday green fee**, with the same shared booking charges noted below.

**Break 90 approach:** For repeat visits, rotate the courses and track where the strokes go. Use the lower fee to play more, rather than making every round a test of your longest tees.

[Official complex overview](https://parks.ny.gov/visit/golf-courses/bethpage-state-park-golf-courses) · [2026 fees](https://parks.ny.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/Bethpage%20State%20Park%20Golf%20Price%20List.pdf)

## 5. James Baird State Park Golf Course

**Pleasant Valley · Best for Hudson Valley golf with a simple fee structure**

Robert Trent Jones designed this relatively flat course on former farmland. It is an appealing public option for a Hudson Valley golf day, with a driving range and practice facilities to fit in a warm-up.

**Price:** 18-hole green fee **$36 weekday / $40 weekend and holiday**. A shared cart adds **$21 per player**, making riding baselines **$57/$61**; a single-rider cart costs more.

**Break 90 approach:** Play toward the side that keeps trouble out of the next shot. Flat terrain does not make every approach equally safe.

[Official course overview and prices](https://parks.ny.gov/visit/golf-courses/james-baird-state-park-golf-course)

## 6. Durand Eastman Golf Course

**Rochester · Best for a particularly low fall fee**

Durand Eastman moves through tree-lined, undulating ground with creeks and ponds. Its park setting and varied lies give it a reason to play beyond the price, while the fall schedule offers a strong walking bargain.

**Price:** **September 30, 2026 to season end: $15 walking / $30 riding for 18 holes**, weekdays and weekends, without a permit discount. These are fall prices, not a promise for next summer.

**Break 90 approach:** Value the fairway over extra distance when a missed drive would leave a blocked approach. Keep the ball in a position you can play from.

[Official course overview](https://monroecountyparksgolf.com/durand-eastman/) · [Dated fall fees](https://monroecountyparksgolf.com/new-rates/)

## 7. Saratoga Spa — Championship Course

**Saratoga Springs · Best for a destination round that still offers value**

Saratoga Spa appears in both of our New York guides because a worthwhile public course does not have to be expensive. Its full 18-hole course is the option referenced here, rather than the separate short course.

**Price:** **September 21, 2026 to close: $36 weekday / $43 weekend and holiday green fee**, with cart **$19 extra**. Peak visitor fees are higher and include nonresident surcharges; confirm the date category.

**Break 90 approach:** Compare the complete round price and available daylight. A reasonably priced morning can be better value than a twilight round you cannot finish.

[Official course website](https://www.saratogaspagolf.com/) · [Seasonal rates](https://www.saratogaspagolf.com/golf/championship-rates)

## 8. En-Joie Golf Club

**Endicott · Best for tournament connections without a resort bill**

The traditional layout beside the Susquehanna offers a useful crossover between destination and value golf. Its rate table rewards choosing the right day and season rather than assuming the most expensive weekend time is the only way to play.

**Price:** **October 12–November 8, 2026:** adult 18-hole riding rates **$52 Monday–Thursday / $58 Friday and holidays / $66 Saturday–Sunday**. Walking equivalents are **$36/$42/$50**. Summer prices differ.

**Break 90 approach:** Use the walking saving if the course and conditions suit you. Keep your pace and routine steady rather than rushing to protect the bargain.

[Official course overview](https://enjoiegolf.com/) · [2026 rate table](https://enjoiegolf.com/1776-2/)

## 9. Churchville Golf Course

**Churchville · Best for another Rochester-area value stop**

Churchville is part of the Monroe County public golf system. It is worth comparing with Durand Eastman when you want an inexpensive golf day near Rochester, with several playing options at the property. Confirm which course and hole count your reservation covers.

**Price:** The county’s **September 30, 2026-to-closing table** lists Churchville East and West at **$15 walking / $30 riding for 18 holes**, or **$12/$20 for nine**. Ask how the available routing is sold for your booking.

**Break 90 approach:** Choose the hole count you actually have time to finish. Nine focused holes are more useful than an unfinished bargain round.

[Official course information](https://monroecountyparksgolf.com/churchville/) · [Current fall fee schedule](https://monroecountyparksgolf.com/new-rates/)

## 10. Turning Stone — Pleasant Knolls

**Verona area · Best for a clearly marked nine-hole alternative**

Pleasant Knolls is a **nine-hole, par-36 recreational course**, separate from Turning Stone’s championship layouts. It rounds out our value list for golfers who want a shorter outing or an additional relaxed round during a resort-area visit.

**Price:** The official page lists a **$19 fee** in its public/resort/rewards category. Confirm the hole count, cart charge and seasonal terms when booking; compare it as a nine-hole outing rather than an 18-hole championship round.

**Break 90 approach:** Give the nine a single scoring goal, such as no penalties or no three-putts. Use the shorter outing to build a repeatable routine.

[Official course and fee information](https://www.turningstone.com/golf/pleasant-knolls)

## Bethpage booking notes

Blue, Green and Yellow do not carry the Black/Red nonresident green-fee distinction. Reservations add **$5 per player**; an optional 18-hole cart is listed at **$44 per cart**. Verified New York residents have a seven-day booking window; visitors have five days. Confirm the current calendar and check-in rules before traveling. [Official reservation instructions](https://www.parks.ny.gov/documents/golf/BethpageBethpageGolfReservationsInstructionsandInformation.pdf).

## Protect the saving

Fall prices are seasonal opportunities, not year-round guarantees. Check aeration, frost policies, course closure dates and daylight. Compare equivalent hole counts and walking or riding totals. At county facilities, confirm which routing you are booking; at resort courses, confirm whether a listed price requires a guest or rewards category.

Pick a manageable scoring goal with our [calculator](/calculator/), then keep the tee selection comfortable. A value round should give you more chances to enjoy golf and improve.
