Four tournaments give golf fans plenty to follow this weekend: a shared lead in Bermuda, a strong LPGA move in South Korea, a consequential DP World Tour card battle, and an opening statement from Darren Clarke.

**Reporting cutoff: October 23, 2026, 8:53 p.m. EDT (America/Detroit).** Scores below describe Friday's completed rounds as reported by the linked sources. They exclude subsequent Saturday play in Asia. Selected names are not full leaderboards; use official scoring for later changes.

## Bermuda: Hirata and Blanchet share the halfway lead

At Port Royal, Kensei Hirata and Chandler Blanchet reached **13 under after two rounds**. Hirata's Friday 64 followed a 65; Blanchet backed up his opening 62 with a 67. Their routes to the same 129 total make an appealing Saturday contrast: one player arriving with Friday momentum, the other preserving the advantage earned on Thursday.

This is a FedExCup Fall event, so the stakes extend beyond Sunday's trophy. Seamus Power, the 2022 champion, remains a particularly interesting player to follow in the fight for next season's playing position.

**Saturday question:** Can either co-leader create separation, or will the chase close the gap? Avoid treating a halfway lead as an invitation to predict a winner.

Sources: [PGA TOUR official scoring](https://www.pgatour.com/tournaments/2026/butterfield-bermuda-championship/R2026528/leaderboard) and [Friday's round report](https://fieldlevelmedia.com/pga_tour/kensei-hirata-joins-chandler-blanchet-atop-bermuda-leaderboard/).

## BMW Ladies Championship: Ryu takes control

Haeran Ryu's **eight-under 64 on Friday** established a two-shot lead after 36 holes at Pine Beach Golf Links. The official leaderboard listed her at **13 under**, with Lauren Coughlin and Minsun Kim at **11 under** ahead of round three.

Ryu's round included two eagles and birdies on her final two holes. That is a substantial scoring burst, but a two-shot margin still leaves a contest with half the tournament remaining.

Chizzy Iwai also made two eagles during a Friday 66, reaching nine under and a tie for sixth. Her move is a reminder to look beyond the final group when choosing someone to follow.

**Saturday question:** Does Ryu sustain the scoring pace, or do the nearest challengers turn this into a tighter Sunday? Our viewing focus is how contenders balance opportunities with the need to avoid one expensive hole.

Sources: [LPGA round-two report](https://www.lpga.com/news/2026/haeran-ryu-takes-a-2-stroke-second-round-lead-at-2026-bmw-ladies-championship), [official leaderboard](https://www.lpga.com/tournaments/bmw-lpga-championship/leaderboard), and [Iwai's Friday recap](https://prod.lpga.com/news/2026/chizzy-iwai-eagles-twice-in-6-under-66-to-climb-to-t6-at-bmw-ladies-championship).

## Genesis Championship: a trophy race and a livelihood race

Casey Jarvis and Antoine Rozner shared the halfway lead at Woo Jeong Hills Country Club. Jarvis shot a bogey-free **67**, while Rozner's bogey-free **65** brought him alongside the South African. We are reporting their confirmed round scores here rather than supplying a tournament total not independently established in this edition.

The other story is playing rights. This is the final event of the DP World Tour regular season, with players around the rankings threshold trying to protect their status. Yuto Katsuragawa's closing birdie helped him reach the weekend; Matteo Manassero and Sean Crocker made the cut on the number. Projected rankings remain projections until the relevant results are settled.

**Saturday question:** Who can improve their position without letting the bigger stakes dictate every decision? Watch both the leaders and the players whose tournament goal is keeping next season's opportunities alive.

Source: [DP World Tour's day-two digest](https://www.europeantour.com/dpworld-tour/news/articles/detail/2026-genesis-championship-day-two-digest/). [Follow official tournament scoring](https://www.europeantour.com/dpworld-tour/).

## Stifel Charity Classic: Clarke opens with 64

PGA TOUR Champions is at the Stifel Charity Classic this weekend, October 23–25. Darren Clarke's opening **64, seven under**, put him at the top of the official Friday leaderboard.

This event is one round behind the three tournaments above: Friday was round one, Saturday is round two, and Sunday is the final round. Comparing its leaders with a 36-hole leaderboard elsewhere would obscure how much golf remains.

**Saturday question:** Can Clarke follow his strong opening with another round that keeps him in control? We have omitted unverified chasing scores rather than fill out a speculative leaderboard.

## What everyday golfers can take from the weekend

Jarvis and Rozner both reached the lead with bogey-free Friday rounds. That does not mean an amateur should expect to eliminate bogeys. Our practical takeaway is narrower: pay attention to the decisions that prevent an ordinary mistake from becoming a much larger score.

Watch a recovery shot, a long first putt, or the choice of target after trouble. Then pick one decision to improve in your own game.
