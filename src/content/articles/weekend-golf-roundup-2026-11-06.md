A three-way tie in Abu Dhabi and a defending champion's surge in Japan give this weekend two very different lead stories. In China, one shot separates the leader from six pursuers. Mexico remains on our watch list, with an important limitation on the scoring we could verify.

**Reporting cutoff: Friday, November 6, 2026, at 8:11 p.m. EST (America/Detroit; November 7 at 01:11 UTC).** Abu Dhabi, Japan and China below use **completed Friday, November 6, second-round snapshots: 36 holes, final for that round, not final tournament results**. Saturday in Asia had already arrived by this cutoff; later Saturday scores are excluded. Mexico's second-round status is provisional as explained below.

## Abu Dhabi: three leaders, McIlroy one behind

**DP World Tour — Abu Dhabi Aldar Championship, Yas Links, November 5–8. Status: Friday round two complete; 36 holes.**

Jon Rahm, Tyrrell Hatton and Francesco Laporta finished Friday level at **12 under**. Hatton's 65 and Rahm's 67 caught Laporta, who followed his opening 62 with a 70. Rory McIlroy's 63 put him just one behind, alongside Adrian Meronk, Sam Bairstow and Matt Jordan. These are selected positions from the [DP World Tour's Friday report](https://www.europeantour.com/dpworld-tour/rolex/news/articles/detail/jon-rahm-and-tyrrell-hatton-join-francesco-laporta-at-abu-dhabi-aldar-championship-summit/), cross-checked against the [PGA TOUR's round report](https://www.pgatour.com/article/news/latest/2026/11/06/rory-mcilroy-makes-11-birdies-in-abu-dhabi-and-trails-leaders-by-one-shot).

**Saturday storyline:** seven players within one shot makes the lead unusually fragile. McIlroy has turned a slow start into a serious challenge, while Laporta has to respond to being caught. With the season nearing its conclusion, the Race to Dubai adds another layer to the tournament contest.

## Japan: Hataoka has the edge over Yamashita

**LPGA — TOTO Japan Classic, Taiheiyo Club Minori Course, Omitama, November 5–8. Status: Friday round two complete; 36 holes.**

Nasa Hataoka shot **64** to reach **12-under 132**, one ahead of Miyu Yamashita. Ana Belac and Fuka Suga shared third at **10 under**, according to the [LPGA's Friday report](https://prod.lpga.com/news/2026/defending-champion-hataoka-takes-a-1-stroke-lead-after-2-rounds-at-lpga-japan). Hataoka's eagle at the 17th helped her complete the back nine in 30.

**Saturday storyline:** the defending champion faces a pursuer arriving with immediate winning momentum. Yamashita won last week's Maybank Championship and followed it with a Friday 67 here; the [LPGA's separate Yamashita report](https://prod.lpga.com/news/2026/miyu-yamashita-sits-solo-second-one-back-at-toto-japan-classic) confirms her one-shot deficit. This is also the final stop of the LPGA's four-event Asian swing. The question is whether Hataoka can build breathing room before the chasing group closes in.

## China: Zeng leads, with six just one shot back

**Ladies European Tour — Aramco China Championship, Mission Hills China, World Cup Course. Status: Friday round two complete; 36 holes.**

Liqi Zeng followed a 65 with a 69 to lead at **12 under**. Charlotte Heath, Mimi Rhodes, Annabell Fuller, Shiyuan Zhou, Luna Sobron Galmes and Ana Peláez Triviño all stood one behind. The [LET's November 6 report](https://live-let.ocs-software.com/blog/zeng-leads-packed-leaderboard-at-midway-mark-of-aramco-china-championship/) confirms those positions.

**Saturday storyline:** Zeng's margin is tiny against such a large chasing group. Rhodes' bogey-free 68 and Zhou's 66 show how quickly someone can move. Watch whether the home leader can separate herself from the pack rather than simply protect one stroke.
