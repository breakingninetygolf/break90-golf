Two season finales have taken very different shapes. Rory McIlroy and Tyrrell Hatton are tied in Dubai, while Stewart Cink has opened a six-shot lead in Phoenix. Three players share the lead at The ANNIKA, and Austin still has to finish its second round.

**Reporting cutoff — Friday, November 13, 2026, at 8:33 p.m. EST (Detroit, 01:33 UTC on November 14).** Overseas standings reflect the close of local Friday play. All five tournaments run November 12–15.

## Dubai — McIlroy and Hatton share the halfway lead

**DP World Tour Championship, Earth Course at Jumeirah Golf Estates. Friday round two complete (36 holes).**

McIlroy birdied the par-5 18th to finish with a 70 and tie Hatton, whose 69 had set the clubhouse target at **5 under**. Race to Dubai leader Eugenio Chacarra was tied for 11th, leaving McIlroy projected to overtake him. See the [DP World Tour’s Friday report](https://www.europeantour.com/dpworld-tour/news/articles/detail/rory-mcilroy-shares-lead-with-tyrrell-hatton-as-race-to-dubai-momentum-swings-in-his-favour/) and [official leaderboard](https://www.europeantour.com/dpworld-tour/dp-world-tour-championship-dubai-2026/leaderboard).

**Saturday watch.** Follow Chacarra’s position alongside the lead pairing. McIlroy is chasing a record-tying eighth season title, and the projection can move even when the leaders stand still.

## Florida — Three tied at The ANNIKA

**LPGA, Pelican Golf Club, Belleair. Friday round two complete (36 holes).**

Carlota Ciganda closed with four straight birdies for a 63. She joined Lindy Duncan (66) and Hye-Jin Choi (67) at **12-under 128**. Mao Saigo and Minami Katsu were two back, while Nelly Korda sat four behind after a 69. The [LPGA leaderboard](https://www.lpga.com/tournaments/the-annika-driven-by-gainbridge-at-pelican/leaderboard) and [Friday round report](https://www.aol.com/articles/hye-jin-chois-wild-day-225816000.html) agree on the lead and margins.

**Saturday watch.** Choi and Duncan are both chasing a first LPGA win, while a Korda victory would secure her final LPGA Hall of Fame point. Watch the Race to CME Globe bubble, too. This is the last chance to move into the top 60 for next week’s finale.

## Austin — Crowe leads as darkness stops play

**PGA TOUR, Austin Championship, Fazio Canyons at Omni Barton Creek. Friday round two suspended and incomplete (provisional).**

Play stopped at **5:37 p.m. CST** with 16 players still on the course. Trace Crowe was **13 under through 16 holes of round two** (34 total). Kevin Yu was in at **12 under** after a second 66. Brian Harman and Kristoffer Ventura had also finished 36 holes at **11 under**. The [PGA TOUR’s suspension report](https://pgatourmedia.pgatourhq.com/static-assets/page/files/tours/2026/pgatour/austinchampionship/roundInfo/PARTIAL%20R2%20Notes.pdf) lists the projected cut at **4 under**. Follow the [official leaderboard](https://www.pgatour.com/tournaments/2026/austin-championship-presented-by-compliance-solutions/R2026558/leaderboard) for the restart.

**Saturday watch.** Crowe has two holes to protect a one-shot edge before the third round. Yu entered Austin at No. 99 in the FedExCup Fall standings. With [Sea Island next week](/articles/fedexcup-fall-finale-preview-2026/), a high finish would give him room above the top-100 eligibility line.

## Phoenix — Cink stretches his lead to six

**PGA TOUR Champions, Charles Schwab Cup Championship, Phoenix Country Club. Friday round two complete (36 holes).**

Stewart Cink backed up his opening 63 with a 65 to reach **14-under 128**. Steve Allan was next at **8 under**, with Zach Johnson, Steven Alker, Alex Cejka and Darren Clarke at **7 under**. The [official Saturday pairing sheet](https://pgatourmedia.pgatourhq.com/static-assets/page/files/tours/2026/pgatourchampions/charlesschwabcupchampionship/roundInfo/R3_Tee_Times_2026_Schwab.pdf), issued Friday evening, records both completed rounds.

**Saturday watch.** Cink began the finale first in the [Schwab Cup standings](https://www.pgatour.com/pgatour-champions/tournaments/2026/charles-schwab-cup-championship/S2026539/schwabcup), just ahead of Johnson. The tournament gap is already six shots. Watch whether Johnson can narrow it while Cink has the chance to defend both the event and season titles.

## Japan — Imahira takes a two-shot lead into Saturday

**Japan Golf Tour, Taiheiyo Masters, Taiheiyo Club Gotemba Course. Local Friday round two complete (36 holes).**

Shugo Imahira’s 64 took him to **10 under**. Tomohiro Ishizaka (64) and Yusaku Miyazato (63) were two back on the [Japan Golf Tour leaderboard](https://www.jgto.org/en/tournament/3038). **Saturday watch.** All three are paired together, so Imahira can see directly whether either challenger repeats Friday’s low scoring.
