Two finales, two different kinds of pressure: the PGA TOUR’s eligibility race reaches its last weekend at Sea Island, while the LPGA’s season ends with A Lim Kim setting the pace in Naples.

**Reporting cutoff: Friday, November 20, 2026, 8:42 p.m. EST (Detroit; November 21, 01:42 UTC).** Scores below are completed local-Friday second-round snapshots, not later live updates. Selected players are listed, rather than full leaderboards.

## Sea Island — Three tied, with more than a trophy at stake

**PGA TOUR, The RSM Classic, November 19–22. Friday round two complete; 36 holes.**

Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen, Marco Penge and Michael Thorbjornsen share the lead at **9-under 133**. Neergaard-Petersen and Penge each shot 65 on Seaside; Thorbjornsen posted 69 on Plantation. Ricky Castillo and Adrien Dumont de Chassart are among the players one shot behind at **8 under**. See the [official leaderboard](https://www.pgatour.com/leaderboard) and [Friday round report](https://www.pgatour.com/article/news/daily-wrapup/2026/11/20/round-2-the-rsm-classic-fedexcup-fall-finale-scores-leaderboard-2027-tour-cards-aon-next-10).

**Saturday watch:** The leaders have very little breathing room. Neergaard-Petersen and Penge are also trying to retain their TOUR cards, adding a second contest to the tournament itself. Watch the eligibility projections alongside the scores as the final PGA TOUR event of the year moves toward its finish. Wind and rain made Friday a demanding test; the question now is who can build separation from a tightly packed chase.

## Naples — Kim’s ace changes the shape of the finale

**LPGA, CME Group Tour Championship, Tiburón Golf Club, November 19–22. Friday round two complete; 36 holes.**

A Lim Kim’s **64** moved her to **15-under 129**, three ahead of Jeeno Thitikul at **12 under**. Kim’s hole-in-one at the par-3 16th supplied the standout moment, followed immediately by a birdie at 17. The [LPGA’s completed-round report](https://prod.lpga.com/news/2026/ace-on-no-16-propels-a-lim-kim-to-second-round-64-at-cme-group-tour-championship) confirms her total; its [Friday wrap-up](https://prod.lpga.com/news/2026/a-lim-kim-makes-hole-in-one-to-surge-into-a-3-shot-lead-in-lpga-season-finale) confirms the three-shot margin. Follow the [official leaderboard](https://www.lpga.com/tournaments/cme-group-tour-championship/leaderboard) for subsequent play.

**Saturday watch:** Kim has earned a cushion, but Thitikul remains close enough to change the picture quickly. The weather is another storyline: the broadcaster announced earlier coverage because of the threat of inclement conditions.

**U.S. Saturday TV, November 21:** [Golf Channel’s revised schedule](https://www.golfchannel.com/lpga/news/cme-group-tour-championship-2026-adjusted-tv-times-tee-times-for-round-3) lists **11 a.m.–2 p.m. EST on CNBC and Golf Channel mobile**, with Golf Channel simulcasting **11 a.m.–1 p.m. EST**. This supersedes the older afternoon listing still visible on the tournament page. Check the broadcaster for further weather changes.

## Elsewhere — A pause after last week’s finales

The DP World Tour’s Dubai finale was **November 12–15**, not this weekend. Its [2027 schedule](https://www.europeantour.com/dpworld-tour/schedule/2027/) begins November 26–29 with the Australian PGA Championship. PGA TOUR Champions also finished its Schwab Cup Championship last weekend; the [official schedule](https://pgatourmedia.pgatourhq.com/schedule?year=2026) places the World Champions Cup in the week beginning November 30. Neither has an active tournament round to recap here.
