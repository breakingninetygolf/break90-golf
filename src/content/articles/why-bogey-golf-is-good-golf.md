## Bogey is the plan

A bogey on every hole is 90 on a par-72 course. Make one par and the card reads 89.

## Protect the next shot

After a poor drive, get back into a playable position instead of trying a miracle recovery.

## Take your medicine

One extra shot is frustrating. Turning it into three extra shots is what wrecks a scorecard.

## Try this for nine holes

Aim for the center of every green and record how many doubles you avoid.
