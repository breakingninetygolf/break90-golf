Georgia’s state-park courses give a value golf trip a useful foundation: scenic settings, several published adult rates around $50–$65, and occasional walking or twilight savings. We add two public-welcome alternatives to compare near LaGrange and Atlanta. This is a value shortlist, not a claim that every course is the cheapest in its region.

These are Break 90 Golf’s research-based editorial selections, not an official ranking or firsthand condition review. We considered course character, booking access and the complete cost of the outing. Separate layouts at one property appear individually.

**Rates and access checked October 15, 2026.** Prices are USD per player unless stated otherwise. Posted rates are references, not guaranteed quotes. Taxes, carts, caddies, gratuities, park entry and lodging are extra unless explicitly included. Where the operator does not publish a dependable current flat rate, we say **quote required** rather than invent a price. Confirm maintenance, seasonal closure and cancellation terms before traveling.

## 1. Arrowhead Pointe Golf Course

**Elberton**

Lake Richard B. Russell frames an unusually scenic state-park round, with ten holes beside the shoreline. The operator’s clear all-in pricing and twilight option make this one of our strongest Georgia value comparisons.

**Price:** **18 holes: $53 weekdays / $64 weekends, including cart, tax and day park pass.** After-2 p.m. twilight is $39/$50. Check walking and nine-hole time restrictions.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Enjoy the lake view without aiming at every waterside flag. Leave a sensible margin for your usual miss.

[Official course information](https://gastateparks.org/ArrowheadPointe)

## 2. The Creek at Hard Labor Creek

**Rutledge / Social Circle area**

Hard Labor Creek makes its strongest budget case for golfers who are comfortable walking. The published walking column is separate from standard pricing, giving you a real way to compare the cost of the complete round rather than relying on a promotional starting number.

**Price:** **18 holes: $51 Monday–Thursday / $62 Friday–Sunday and holidays, plus tax. Walking: $34/$39 plus tax.** Confirm cart inclusion in the standard quote and any park-entry charge.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Walk only if the terrain and weather fit you. Keep enough energy for solid contact late in the round.

[Official course information](https://gastateparks.org/TheCreek)

## 3. Highland Walk Golf Course

**Royston**

Steep rolling ground, crowned fairways and sloping greens give Highland Walk its character. It earns a place because the course asks interesting questions at a state-park price; it is not a promise of an easy walking round.

**Price:** **Published 18-hole rate: $51 weekdays / $60 weekends.** Friday and holidays use weekend rates. Confirm tax and cart treatment at checkout.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Choose your target with the slope in mind. Ask about cart rules and pace before assuming you can comfortably walk it.

[Official course information](https://gastateparks.org/HighlandWalk)

## 4. Brazell's Creek Golf Course

**Reidsville**

Brazell’s Creek offers a distinctive contrast between tree-lined original holes and a more open back nine connected across the wetlands. Five tee sets help a group find a manageable version of this par-71 layout.

**Price:** **18 holes: $47 weekdays / $52 weekends, including tax.** Confirm golf-cart inclusion. The separate $10 FootGolf rate is not an 18-hole golf green fee.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Pick a tee that suits the wetland carry. Check wind on the open holes instead of repeating the same club choice automatically.

[Official course information](https://gastateparks.org/BrazellsCreek)

## 5. Meadow Links Golf Course

**Fort Gaines**

Willard Byrd’s layout uses rolling countryside, large greens and open-looking views for a different state-park experience. Its flat weekday/weekend published green-fee figure is useful when your only available golf day is Saturday.

**Price:** **18 holes: $51 weekdays and weekends, including tax.** Confirm whether your quote includes a cart and any park-entry charge.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Use the width sensibly and pay attention to the large greens. A long first putt still needs a clear distance target.

[Official course information](https://gastateparks.org/MeadowLinks)

## 6. The Lakes Golf Course at Laura S. Walker

**Waycross**

The Lakes adds a southeast Georgia choice near the Okefenokee area. Native sand, three lakes and several tee options make it a useful alternative to a coastal-resort bill when the goal is simply another enjoyable full round.

**Price:** **18 holes: $52 weekdays / $62.83 weekends, including cart and tax.** Check availability and any separate park-access requirements.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Choose a tee that keeps the water carries realistic. On sandy misses, first plan a clean escape.

[Official course information](https://gastateparks.org/thelakes)

## 7. Wallace Adams Golf Course

**McRae-Helena**

Wallace Adams gives this list a South Georgia park-and-lodge option. Pines, willows and magnolias surround the classic 18-hole layout. It is worth pricing for a relaxed trip that combines golf with an overnight stop rather than a luxury-resort stay.

**Price:** **Current day rate: quote required.** Ask for the 18-hole riding total and compare with the lodge’s golf packages. No old promotional rate is presented as current.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Choose a comfortable target through the trees. Keep your recovery simple if a tee shot leaves the fairway.

[Official course information](https://littleocmulgeelodge.com/little-ocmulgee-golf/)

## 8. Georgia Veterans Memorial Golf Course

**Cordele**

The golf course at Lake Blackshear Resort adds an inland south-central option with lodging and outdoor activities nearby. It belongs on the value comparison as a park-based golf getaway, with the final decision depending on the actual quote.

**Price:** **Current day rate: quote required.** Use the official tee-time booking or request a complete golf-and-lodging package; confirm cart and tax.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Do not let a package determine your tee choice. Play the distance that leaves you familiar approach shots.

[Official course information](https://www.lakeblackshearresort.com/golf/) · [Official rates / booking](https://www.lakeblackshearresort.com/golf/book-tee-times/)

## 9. The Fields Golf Club

**LaGrange**

The Fields is a public-welcome, links-style choice in west Georgia, with a rolling landscape and a different feel from the state-park group above. Compare its day quote when you want course character without organizing an entire resort visit.

**Price:** **Current public rate: quote required.** Ask the club for weekday, weekend and walking options; older winter-rate pages are not used as a current price.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Pay attention to how far the ball can run. Choose a landing area rather than judging every shot only by carry distance.

[Official course information](https://www.thefieldsgolfclub.com/)

## 10. Cobblestone Golf Course

**Acworth**

Cobblestone sits beside Lake Acworth and gives the Atlanta-area golfer a practical local option to price against a longer destination trip. Its inclusion is about the complete outing: an appealing public setting without adding overnight lodging by default.

**Price:** **Live public tee-time quote required.** Compare the selected date and time; Cobb Card discounts are separate from an ordinary visitor quote.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Check water positions before choosing the tee club. A useful approach angle is often worth more than maximum distance.

[Official course information](https://www.cobblestonegolf.com/course-information/) · [Official rates / booking](https://www.cobblestonegolf.com/tee-times/)

## Plan a round that fits your trip

Choose a region before choosing ten tee times. Coastal Georgia, Lake Oconee, the Atlanta area and Lookout Mountain are different trips. A cheaper green fee can stop being good value after a long transfer. Around Masters week, confirm special rates and restrictions rather than applying an ordinary weekday price.

Before booking, ask about green aeration, the tees and holes currently available, cart restrictions, the full checkout total and cancellation terms. Confirm that the booking is for the course named here when a property has multiple layouts.

For your score, use the [math of breaking 90](/articles/break-90-golf-scoring-math-find-lost-strokes/) and choose tees that fit your normal carry distance. A memorable round does not require playing the back tees.
