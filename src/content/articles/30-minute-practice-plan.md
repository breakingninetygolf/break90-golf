## Minutes 0–10: Short putts

Make putts from three to five feet with the same routine each time.

## Minutes 10–20: Lag putting and chips

Split time between long putts and basic chips to a landing spot.

## Minutes 20–30: Tee-shot targets

Pick a fairway-width target and alternate clubs as if playing holes.

## Keep score

Count successful reps and repeat weekly.
