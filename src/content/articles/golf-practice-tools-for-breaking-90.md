## 1. SKLZ Golf Alignment Sticks: give your setup a reference

Alignment sticks let you compare where you think you are aiming with a visible line. The SKLZ set is one option for building that reference into range sessions.

**Who should consider them:** golfers who want a repeatable way to check aim and ball position before working on their swing.

**Try this drill:** lay a stick flat on the ground outside the hitting area, parallel to your intended target line. Set the clubface toward the target, then take your stance. Hit five easy shots and repeat with a different target. Keep the stick clear of the clubhead's path and follow the range's rules.

**The limitation:** a straighter setup does not automatically fix a slice or hook. Use the sticks to check setup, not to force your swing around an obstacle.

**Skip the purchase if:** a spare club placed safely on the ground already gives you the reference you need.

## 2. PuttOUT Premium Pressure Putt Trainer: put a goal into short-putt practice

PuttOUT's trainer combines a folding ramp with a small target designed to reward a particular pace and line. That gives an at-home session a more defined task than rolling balls across the carpet without keeping score.

**Who should consider it:** golfers with a suitable rolling surface who want a compact target for short-putt sessions.

**Try this drill:** start three feet away. Roll ten putts using the same routine and record how many reach the target on your intended line. Repeat from four feet. Compare sessions from the same positions rather than changing the challenge every day.

**The limitation:** the surface matters. An uneven carpet can distort feedback, and a straight indoor putt does not teach green reading. Keep some practice time for a real putting green.

**Skip the purchase if:** a coin target and a simple gate made from two tees already keep your practice focused.

## 3. Callaway Chip Shot Chipping Net: make your target obvious

A chipping net gives you a visible destination and helps structure a short session. Callaway's Chip Shot is a portable option to consider for that role.

**Who should consider it:** golfers who have a safe practice space and are more likely to practice when a target is already set up.

**Try this drill:** use one club and one comfortable distance. Hit ten controlled chips toward the target, record your successes, then repeat. Change the distance only after completing the set so you can compare like with like.

**The limitation:** catching a ball in a net is different from landing it on a green and judging its release. Use a practice green when you want to work on rollout. A small chipping target is not a full-swing containment net; use an appropriate safe space and follow the product instructions.

**Skip the purchase if:** a towel landing target at an appropriate practice area does the job.
