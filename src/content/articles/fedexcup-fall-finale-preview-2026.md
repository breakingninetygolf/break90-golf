The most revealing leaderboard in November might be the one showing who will have a place to play next year. The FedExCup Fall is heading toward its finish at Sea Island, where a birdie can change a player's schedule as much as his pay cheque.

**Preview checked November 7, 2026, at 9:50 p.m. EST (America/Detroit).** Dates and announced commitments below are a planning snapshot. Rankings use the official starting column for this week's Mexico event, not changing in-tournament projections. This preview is separate from our [November 6 Weekend Watch](/articles/weekend-golf-roundup-2026-11-06/).

Think of Sea Island as the season's last full-field points event, with several different contests happening inside one tournament.</p></div>

The [official TOUR Championship results](https://www.pgatour.com/tournaments/2026/the-tour-championship/R2026060) list Scottie Scheffler as the champion at East Lake, August 27–30. The coming Fall finish is its own consequential chapter.

## RSM Classic: the essentials

The [PGA TOUR's event overview](https://www.pgatour.com/tournaments/2026/the-rsm-classic/R2026493/overview) confirms **November 19–22**, a **$7.4 million purse**, and defending champion **Sami Välimäki**, who won in 2025 at 23 under. Seaside is listed as a par 70, 7,157 yards; both Seaside and Plantation are part of the event.

Tournament week runs **November 16–22**, with practice rounds, Wednesday's pro-am, then four competition rounds, according to [Sea Island's event information](https://www.seaisland.com/experiences/special-events/the-rsm-classic/). Davis Love III hosts the tournament through the Davis Love Foundation. The Sunday trophy presentation follows play on the 18th green.

**Why this setting matters:** [Sea Island describes Seaside](https://www.seaisland.com/golf/courses/seaside/) as an exposed links-style layout with dunes, bunkers and wind that can alter the challenge each day. Our viewing focus is distance control and approaches in that wind: a player can be comfortable on the tournament board and still feel every shot on the eligibility board. That is a storyline to watch, rather than a weather forecast or a claim about anyone's tactics.

## How the FedExCup Fall works, in plain English

The Fall continues the points story for players who finished outside the Playoffs' top 50. Their earlier work still counts: regular-season points and points from the FedEx St. Jude Championship carry forward. The top 50 can enter Fall tournaments, but their Fall results do not add to this eligibility race. That distinction comes from the [PGA TOUR's FedExCup Fall explainer](https://www.pgatour.com/article/news/how-it-works/fedexcup-fall-playoffs-standings-how-it-works-51-70-125).

Winning brings its own route to security: a Fall title carries a two-year TOUR exemption and 500 points, plus applicable championship invitations. Those benefits help explain why an already-exempt player still has reason to enter.

The three numbers to remember are **60, 100 and 125**. They are different thresholds, not three versions of the same cut.

| Group or final Fall position | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Top 50 through the Playoffs | Full-field and Signature Event access already secured for the following season. |
| Nos. 51–70 entering the Fall | Full-field eligibility already protected; still chasing extra Signature Event opportunities. |
| Final Fall Nos. 51–60 | The Aon Next 10: two early-season Signature Event invitations. |
| Final Fall top 100 | The key full-field exemption line, including THE PLAYERS. |
| Final Fall Nos. 101–125 | Conditional status rather than the same full-field access as the top 100, unless otherwise exempt. |

The [2026 PGA TOUR media guide, FedExCup overview](https://pgatourmedia.pgatourhq.com/static-assets/page/files/mediaguides/2026%20Media%20Guide_01.26.pdf) explains the protected top-50/top-70 groups and the Aon Next 10. The [current eligibility guide](https://www.pgatour.com/eligibility) sets out the top-100 outcome. The linked explainer describes conditional status for Nos. 101–125, though its season-specific examples still refer to 2026; this November's race concerns **2027**.

A player outside the top 100 may still have another exemption. Equally, finishing inside 125 should not be described as keeping an unrestricted card. Check the player's eligibility category alongside his rank.

## What happens after the finale?

RSM closes this full-field points run, but qualifying remains. [Final Stage of PGA TOUR Q-School](https://www.pgatour.com/article/news/how-it-works/how-it-works-2026-pga-tour-q-school-presented-by-korn-ferry) is **December 10–13**, at TPC Sawgrass' Dye's Valley and Sawgrass Country Club. Five finishers earn 2027 TOUR membership; ties affecting the final places are resolved by a playoff. There are also separate pathways from the Korn Ferry Tour and DP World Tour.

Our watch list for Sea Island is simple: follow the trophy chase, the **60 line**, and the **100 line** together. Then look at who actually holds an exemption before interpreting the consequences. That is where the final Sunday becomes more than one winner and one score.
