Georgia offers coastal marsh golf, Lookout Mountain scenery, Lake Oconee resort rounds and public courses near Atlanta. This shortlist is for a special golf trip where price is secondary. Public-access here includes resort courses available through an eligible stay; it does not mean every course sells an unrestricted day tee time.

These are Break 90 Golf’s research-based editorial selections, not an official ranking or firsthand condition review. We considered course character, booking access and the variety of the golf experience. Separate layouts at one property appear individually.

**Rates and access checked October 15, 2026.** Prices are USD per player unless stated otherwise. Posted rates are references, not guaranteed quotes. Taxes, carts, caddies, gratuities, park entry and lodging are extra unless explicitly included. Where the operator does not publish a dependable current flat rate, we say **quote required** rather than invent a price. Confirm maintenance, seasonal closure and cancellation terms before traveling.

## 1. Sea Island — Seaside Course

**St. Simons Island**

Seaside supplies the coastal-marsh setting that makes a Georgia golf trip feel different from a routine inland round. Tidal creeks, dunes and wind create the setting for a course that shares hosting duties for the RSM Classic.

**Price:** **Resort golf quote required.** Request green fees, accommodation requirements and caddie charges together; no verified standalone flat rate is quoted.

**Access:** Resort-guest access; arrange an eligible stay and confirm tee times before booking lodging.

**Break 90 approach:** Check the wind before selecting a target. Aim for the safe section of the green rather than chasing an exposed flag.

[Official course information](https://www.seaisland.com/golf/)

## 2. McLemore — The Keep

**Rising Fawn**

*Map marker: shared McLemore clubhouse/check-in area. The Keep is a separate nearby course; confirm arrival instructions with the resort.*

The Keep is a mountain-destination choice on Lookout Mountain. Its site and broad landscape make it a reason to build a trip around northwest Georgia rather than simply adding a round to an Atlanta visit.

**Price:** **Access-specific quote required.** Confirm the golf charge, cart/caddie fees and any stay requirement directly with McLemore.

**Access:** Access through eligible resort/stay-and-play arrangements, membership or limited Friends of McLemore opportunities; confirm eligibility.

**Break 90 approach:** Keep a margin for wind and unfamiliar elevation. Play the tee that gives you repeatable carries.

[Official course information](https://www.themclemore.com/golf/the-keep-at-mclemore)

## 3. Reynolds Lake Oconee — Great Waters

**Eatonton / Lake Oconee**

Jack Nicklaus’s Great Waters uses the Lake Oconee setting as the central reason to visit. It is a good fit for a resort trip that combines golf with time on the lake, with the course itself a priority rather than a spare activity.

**Price:** **Resort package or eligible guest quote required.** Confirm the course-specific golf charge, lodging, cart and any caddie costs.

**Access:** Resort-guest access; arrange an eligible stay and confirm tee times before booking lodging.

**Break 90 approach:** Choose a landing area with room on the safe side of the water. One conservative shot can keep a big number off the card.

[Official course information](https://www.reynoldslakeoconee.com/life/golf/great-waters)

## 4. Sea Island — Plantation Course

**St. Simons Island**

Plantation offers a second championship experience at Sea Island, reworked by Mark and Davis Love III. Together with Seaside it lets a group compare two layouts from one base rather than spending the trip moving between distant regions.

**Price:** **Resort golf quote required.** Confirm course availability around events, the eligible accommodation and the full golf/caddie total.

**Access:** Resort-guest access; arrange an eligible stay and confirm tee times before booking lodging.

**Break 90 approach:** Treat it as a fresh course, not a replay of Seaside. Use a reliable tee club and learn the green pace early.

[Official course information](https://www.seaisland.com/golf/)

## 5. McLemore — Highlands Course

**Rising Fawn**

Highlands is the other principal course in McLemore’s mountain offering, shaped by Bill Bergin and Rees Jones. It is a distinctive companion to The Keep and a useful reason to stay long enough to play two different rounds.

**Price:** **Access-specific quote required.** Ask separately for Highlands availability and the complete stay-and-play or Friends rate.

**Access:** Eligible resort/stay-and-play guests and Friends of McLemore may arrange play; confirm the applicable booking rules.

**Break 90 approach:** Choose targets with the slope and the next lie in mind. An unfamiliar downhill shot is a reason for more margin, not less.

[Official course information](https://www.themclemore.com/golf/highlands-course)

## 6. Old Toccoa Farm

**Mineral Bluff**

Old Toccoa Farm brings mountain golf to the Blue Ridge area with a clear published day-rate schedule. It is a practical alternative for travelers who want a standout North Georgia round and prefer to see the golf total before building the rest of the trip.

**Price:** **18 holes: $115 Monday, Wednesday and Thursday; $180 Friday–Sunday.** Includes green fee, cart, range balls and taxes. Closed Tuesdays according to the checked schedule.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Choose tees for the terrain as well as yardage. Use the included practice time to find a dependable first tee shot.

[Official course information](https://oldtoccoafarm.com/golf/)

## 7. The Club at Savannah Harbor

**Savannah / Hutchinson Island**

Savannah Harbor combines tidal-wetland surroundings with views toward historic Savannah. It fits a city-and-golf trip where dining and sightseeing matter alongside the round, making it a different choice from an isolated mountain resort.

**Price:** **Live tee-time quote required.** Compare the official golf booking total with any Westin stay-and-play package; confirm cart, tax and maintenance status.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Keep the ball in play around the wetlands. Select a target that gives your normal miss somewhere to finish.

[Official course information](https://www.theclubatsavannahharbor.com/)

## 8. Barnsley Resort — Fazio Course

**Adairsville**

The Jim Fazio course gives North Georgia travelers a resort setting without making an overnight stay the only access route: the operator welcomes day golfers. It is a useful choice when your group wants a substantial golf day near Adairsville.

**Price:** **Day-golf or stay-and-play quote required.** Ask for the 18-hole total and whether cart, practice access, tax or resort charges are included.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Use a comfortable club from the tee and avoid compounding an awkward lie with a low-percentage recovery.

[Official course information](https://www.barnsleyresort.com/recreation/golf/)

## 9. Reynolds Lake Oconee — Oconee Course

**Greensboro**

Rees Jones’s Oconee course gives a Reynolds trip a second lakeside design, with risk-and-reward choices near the shoreline. It is worth comparing with Great Waters rather than assuming every course on a resort property plays the same way.

**Price:** **Eligible resort-guest quote required.** Request the Oconee course specifically and confirm the package, cart and caddie terms.

**Access:** Resort-guest access; arrange an eligible stay and confirm tee times before booking lodging.

**Break 90 approach:** Pick the safer route when the carry sits at the edge of your normal distance. A routine bogey keeps the round moving.

[Official course information](https://www.reynoldslakeoconee.com/life/golf/the-oconee)

## 10. Stone Mountain Golf Club — Stonemont Course

**Stone Mountain**

Robert Trent Jones Sr.’s Stonemont gives the shortlist a public-golf option near Atlanta. It is a traditional test to compare with the club’s different Lakemont layout, particularly when the trip does not allow a coastal or mountain-resort detour.

**Price:** **Live tee-time quote required.** Confirm Stonemont rather than Lakemont, cart inclusion and any separate Stone Mountain Park entry/parking charge.

**Access:** Public tee times; reserve ahead.

**Break 90 approach:** Read the hole guide before you arrive. Choose the widest useful landing area rather than trying to overpower a narrow target.

[Official course information](https://www.stonemountaingolf.com/about-the-courses/)

## Plan a round that fits your trip

Choose a region before choosing ten tee times. Coastal Georgia, Lake Oconee, the Atlanta area and Lookout Mountain are different trips. A cheaper green fee can stop being good value after a long transfer. Around Masters week, confirm special rates and restrictions rather than applying an ordinary weekday price.

Before booking, ask about green aeration, the tees and holes currently available, cart restrictions, the full checkout total and cancellation terms. Confirm that the booking is for the course named here when a property has multiple layouts.

For your score, use the [math of breaking 90](/articles/break-90-golf-scoring-math-find-lost-strokes/) and choose tees that fit your normal carry distance. A memorable round does not require playing the back tees.
