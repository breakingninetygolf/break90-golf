## Measure the room before ordering

For the MLM2PRO, Rapsodo specifies **6.5–8.5 feet behind the ball**, plus **8 feet from the ball to the net** indoors. Those components add up to approximately **14.5–16.5 feet from the unit to the net**. That is tracking depth, not a complete room-safety specification. Allow for the enclosure, space behind the net, full swing clearance and other requirements in the current instructions.

## A practice session that can help your course decisions

Use the first sessions to build a small, honest carry chart. Keep conditions consistent, use the correct setup and record the ball type. Range-ball results may differ from the ball you play on the course.

1. **Warm up first.** Do not use your first stiff swings as your distance baseline.
2. **Pick three familiar clubs.** Hit ten normal shots with each. Keep ordinary mishits in your notes; label obvious misreads separately.
3. **Record typical carry and the spread.** A median or middle group is more useful for planning than the longest shot. Note short misses and the common left/right pattern.
4. **Change the target.** Hit one shot at a time with your normal routine, rather than grooving the same club endlessly.
5. **Recheck on the course.** Use real results to refine the chart. Wind, lie, temperature and elevation still matter.
