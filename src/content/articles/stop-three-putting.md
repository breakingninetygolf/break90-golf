## The first putt is a speed problem

From long range, prioritize leaving a manageable second putt rather than trying to make everything.

## The three-foot circle

Practice lag putts that finish inside an imaginary three-foot circle around the cup.

## One useful drill

Hit three putts each from 15, 25 and 35 feet. Count how many finish in the circle.

## Track the outcome

Record three-putts after every round and watch the trend.
