## Start with the math

On a par-72 course, 89 can be 17 bogeys and one par. That is not flawless golf. It is golf without a pile of doubles and triples.

## Choose a safe target

Aim for the widest part of the fairway and the middle of the green. You do not need to attack every flag.

## Know when to put driver away

If trouble squeezes the landing area, a shorter club that stays in play may save more shots than an extra 30 yards.

## Your next-round assignment

Track penalty strokes, three-putts, and doubles. Practice the category costing you the most.
